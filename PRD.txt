# Arabic Procurement Intelligence Platform (APIP)

## Product Vision

An AI-powered platform that helps Arabic-speaking companies, freelancers, and startups analyze procurement documents, tenders, contracts, and RFPs in minutes instead of hours.

The platform combines Hybrid RAG, Multi-Agent Workflows, Document Intelligence, and Production AI Engineering practices to extract insights, identify risks, answer questions, and generate proposal drafts.

---

# Problem Statement

Organizations regularly receive:

* Tenders
* Procurement documents
* Government RFPs
* Contracts
* Vendor requirements

These documents are often:

* Long (50-300+ pages)
* Complex
* Written in formal Arabic or English
* Difficult to review manually

Users spend hours trying to understand:

* What is required
* Submission deadlines
* Required documents
* Eligibility criteria
* Risks and blockers
